How Exchanges Work

  1. Submit Request: Fill out this form and upload clear images of the product.
  2. Approval: Our team will review your request. Once approved, you will be notified.
  3. Ship It Back: You will be responsible for shipping the item back to us via any courier of your choice.
  4. Submit Tracking: After shipping, you MUST enter your courier's Tracking ID on the next page.
  5. Inspection & Replacement: Once we receive the item, it will undergo a physical inspection. We will ship your replacement if it passes. Note: If the item is not in good condition or appears used, the exchange will be rejected and the original product will be sent back to you.
